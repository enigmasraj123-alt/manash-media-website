What core services does MANASH specialize in?

How does MANASH handle social media content management?

add

We manage your complete organic social presence — from strategy and content scheduling to community engagement and performance reporting — ensuring consistent brand voice and growing an active, loyal audience.

Do you edit the video, or just run the ads?

add

Both. Our editors cut raw footage into publish-ready reels, shorts, and ad creatives, paced for retention — so the assets fuelling your paid campaigns and organic feed come from the same team, on the same brief.

add

Initial campaign tracking and audience data normalize within 7 to 14 days. During this window, we aggressively test ad creatives and targeting strategies to optimize for maximum return on ad spend (ROAS).

add

We offer flexible monthly retainers tailored to your current scale and budget, allowing us to pivot strategies as your ad spend grows without locking you into rigid multi-year agreements.

add

We begin with a deep dive into your target audience, past ad performance, and current social presence to build a custom roadmap that aligns paid acquisition, organic content, and video production.
